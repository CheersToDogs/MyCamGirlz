When you cancel, your paid access continues through the end of the current billing period that has already been paid for. You will not be charged again after cancellation.

Subscription fees are charged for access that is provided immediately upon payment. Refunds are generally not provided for partial or unused portions of a billing period.

If you believe you were charged in error, charged more than once, or charged without authorization, contact us promptly at support@mycamgirlz.com. 6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback so we can resolve the issue directly. Filing a chargeback without first contacting us may result in termination of your account.

7. Free Accounts

Free accounts incur no charge and require no cancellation. To delete a free account, email support@mycamgirlz.com.
